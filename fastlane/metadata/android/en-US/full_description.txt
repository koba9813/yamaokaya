Yamaokaya is Doko? is a free and open-source Android app that shows the distance and direction from your current location to the nearest Yamaokaya ramen shop.

Features:
- Real-time distance and bearing to the nearest store
- A ramen image in the center points toward the store
- Persistent notification with the nearest store distance (background location)
- Special effects and notification when you get within 50 m
- Stamp rally / check-in history
- Share your distance to the store

Notes:
- Only stores registered inside the app are searchable
- Search radius is 100 km
- "Allow all the time" location permission is required for background notifications
- A notification is shown continuously while the distance tracker is running
- This is an unofficial fan app and is not affiliated with Yamaokaya
